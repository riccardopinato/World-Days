## Distinctions and unresolved facts

- World Duchenne Awareness Day: campaign preceded the **2023** UN designation; UN day starts **2024**. Do not confuse formal recognition with the patient's movement's beginning.
- Police Cooperation Day: 7 September is the **1923** creation of INTERPOL's predecessor; UN institution occurred in **2022**, first UN edition in **2023**.
- South-South Cooperation Day: **2003** UN establishment used **19 December**; the **2011** General Assembly decision moved it to **12 September starting in 2012**, recalling the 1978 Buenos Aires Plan of Action.
- Caves and Karst: UNESCO first scheduled international celebration in **September 2026** in Postojna, Slovenia; the exact calendar-day rationale and individual founder are not established by the primary institutional page reviewed.
- Interventional Cardiology: **16 September 1977** first coronary angioplasty by Andreas Grüntzig is the reason for the anniversary, separate from the **2022** UN proclamation.
- Ozone Layer: **16 September 1987** Montreal Protocol signature explains the date, **1994** UN resolution and **1995** first UN annual day.
- Sign Languages: World Federation of the Deaf founded **23 September 1951**; WFD proposed the celebration and Antigua and Barbuda sponsored the UN resolution; first UN observance **2018**.
- Universal Access to Information: UNESCO proclamation **2015** / first UNESCO celebration **2016** versus separate UN recognition **2019**.
- World Cotton Day: first WTO event **2019** initiated by Cotton-4, distinct from official UN recognition **2021**.
- Poverty Eradication Day: first Trocadéro gathering **1987** and UN resolution **1992**; the 17 October anniversary is individually traceable.
- Dates whose historical reasons or originators lack reliable first-party evidence remain explicitly **UNKNOWN**; no unverified narrative may be promoted by automatic tooling.
