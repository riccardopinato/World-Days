## Long description — English master

Discover what the world observes today with World Days, a multilingual
calendar of international observances and selected national commemorations.

Explore today's dates, browse the calendar, search by name or category,
and learn which organizations recognize an observance. Verified source
information is shown separately from curated historical articles, so you
can understand what is documented without mixing official recognition
with popular observances.

Save interesting days, discover upcoming observances and share original
illustrated day cards. Optional local reminders and a home-screen widget
help you return on the dates that matter to you.

The bundled core catalog works offline. Additional verified content
can be downloaded securely when available. World Days supports English,
Italian, Spanish, French, German and Portuguese.

The core app is free. A single advertising banner may appear after
privacy choices; an optional one-time purchase removes ads.

World Days is an independent informational product and is not an
official United Nations, UNESCO, WHO or government application.
