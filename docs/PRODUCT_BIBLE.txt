1. PRODUCT
Name: World Days
Store subtitle: Discover what the world celebrates today
Positioning: visual, daily-return consumer app for international/world observances.
Primary language: English.
Launch locales: en, it, es, fr, de, pt.
Platforms: Android, iOS, Flutter Web Preview.
Core promise: open the app and understand in seconds what is observed today, why it exists, who recognizes it, and what is coming next.

2. PRODUCT PRINCIPLES
- English master content; translations are derived from the canonical English editorial record.
- Local-first: bundled catalog works offline.
- No login required in v1.
- Transparent provenance: each observance has authority/source and officiality.
- Official, institutional and popular observances are never flattened into one undifferentiated list.
- Very visual editorial UI, but lightweight assets and no local AI model.
- Retention comes from useful daily discovery, not permission walls or ad friction.
- Ads never block core use.

5. SOURCE AUTHORITY
Tier A — canonical primary:
- United Nations Observances / resolutions
- UNESCO International Days
- WHO official global public-health days/weeks
- specialized UN agencies where they are the establishing authority (FAO, ILO, ITU, WMO, WIPO, IMO, UN Tourism, etc.)

Tier B — institutional/cross-check:
- UNRIC calendar
- Council of Europe
- EU/national institutional sources

Tier C — editorial discovery:
- giornatemondiali.it
- Rai Scuola
- Pineta Nature Resort calendar
- Wikipedia lists

Conflict rule: Tier A wins. Tier C never upgrades an observance to “official”.
Editorial prose/images from third-party sites are not copied; World Days produces original copy and keeps source/provenance metadata.

6. DATASET ACCEPTANCE — 1 JANUARY TO 31 DECEMBER
- Every calendar date is navigable, including dates with zero official observances.
- 100% of the current UN list of International Days and Weeks is represented.
- UNESCO-only and WHO-official observances not already represented are merged and de-duplicated.
- User-provided sources are processed as discovery candidates, then verified/classified before publication.
- Fixed and movable observances use recurrence rules, not hard-coded pseudo-fixed dates.
- Weeks/months can overlap day observances.
- Every published observance has at least one source; official badges require primary authority evidence.
- Duplicate semantic observances are merged using aliases, not duplicated.
- Dataset ships versioned and records last verification date.
- Leap day supported.
- Tests generate occurrences for at least 2026, 2027 and 2028.

8. VISUAL SYSTEM
Brand direction: editorial calendar + globe, colorful and modern, not generic Material UI.
Use Material 3 only as technical foundation; custom hero cards, category gradients, editorial typography, large date treatment, swipe transitions and locally-generated share cards define the consumer identity.
Use reusable visual_key/category illustration packs rather than one heavy image per observance.
Target lightweight ARM64 build; no local LLM/model pack.

10. MONETIZATION
AdMob mobile only; Web Preview uses no-op/mock boundary.
Primary formats:
- Large Anchored Adaptive banner in dedicated safe slots
- Inline Adaptive ads in long discovery/calendar feeds
- Interstitial only at natural completion points such as a completed quiz/session, with internal frequency caps
Do not show interstitials on launch, tab switch, Back, article open, settings, or notification deep-link.
Debug/QA/manual testing uses test ads only.
UMP consent is refreshed each launch and ad requests wait for canRequestAds().
No-Ads purchase target: lifetime EUR 2.99 (final store price can be localized).
No core content paywall in v1.

Primary KPIs: D1/D7/D30 retention, DAU/MAU, sessions/DAU, observances opened/session, notification opt-in and tap rate, favorite/share rate, quiz completion, ad impressions/DAU, ARPDAU, crash-free users.

15. STEP 0/2 IMPLEMENTATION FREEZE — 2026-10-11
- Package/bundle baseline: com.riccardopinato.world_days
- Canonical recurrence field is recurrenceRules[] (plural), because one observance can occur more than once per year.
- First-party recurrence engine chosen instead of adding full RFC5545 `rrule` dependency.
- Deterministic supported rules: FIXED_DATE, NTH_WEEKDAY, LAST_WEEKDAY, LAST_DAY_OF_MONTH, FIXED_RANGE, MULTI_RULE; LUNAR/AUTHORITY_DEFINED require verified yearly overrides.
- Official core seed v2026.10.11.1: 268 canonical observances assembled from current UN core plus verified UNESCO-only / WHO-official additions in this pass.
- 2026 occurrence index: 269 occurrences across 365 dates; 221 dates have >=1 current official/institutional observance and 144 are currently empty at the official-core layer.
- 2026 deterministic recurrence vectors PASS.
- Cross-year vectors PASS for 2026/2027/2028: International Day of Cooperatives, World Habitat Day, World Migratory Bird Day, World Maritime Day, World Day of Remembrance for Road Traffic Victims, World Philosophy Day, International Girls in ICT Day.
- Vesak remains authority/lunar override based: 2026 resolved from current UN calendar; 2027/2028 deliberately unresolved until primary yearly evidence is added.
- Discovery backlog v0.1 contains 156 Pineta 2026 raw candidates not promoted to app-facing canonical truth until verification/dedup/classification.
