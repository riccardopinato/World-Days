WORLD DAYS — SOURCE REGISTRY v0.1
Verified: 2026-10-11

TIER A — PRIMARY / CANONICAL
A01 United Nations — List of International Days and Weeks
URL: https://www.un.org/en/observances/list-days-weeks
Role: canonical UN observance list; title, date/rule, resolution/agency marker.
Status: ACTIVE / PRIMARY

A02 UNESCO — International Days
URL: https://www.unesco.org/en/days
List: https://www.unesco.org/en/days/list
Role: UNESCO-only observances and UNESCO authority verification.
Status: ACTIVE / PRIMARY
Verified examples during this pass: World Logic Day (14 Jan), World Art Day (15 Apr), World Metrology Day (20 May), International Day for Biosphere Reserves (3 Nov), International Day of Intangible Cultural Heritage (17 Oct), World Coding Day (29 Oct).

A03 WHO — Global health days and weeks
URL: https://www.who.int/campaigns
Role: WHO official global public-health days/weeks and campaign pages.
Status: ACTIVE / PRIMARY
WHO explicitly distinguishes 13 official days + 2 official weeks from other health observances.

TIER B — INSTITUTIONAL / CROSS-CHECK
B01 UNRIC Italia — Giornate internazionali ONU
URL: https://unric.org/it/giornate-internazionali-onu/
Role: Italian institutional cross-check, localized discovery and links back to UN system sources.

B02 Council of Europe — Human Rights Calendar
URL: https://www.coe.int/it/web/compass/human-rights-calendar
Role: human-rights/regional observance discovery and educational context.
Important: not treated as an exhaustive or definitive global list.

TIER C — DISCOVERY / EDITORIAL
C01 Giornatemondiali.it
URL: https://www.giornatemondiali.it/
Role: candidate discovery, Italian search vocabulary, category expectations.
Important: its static list explicitly omits some movable observances; never canonical for recurrence.

C02 Rai Scuola — Giornate nazionali e internazionali
URL: https://www.raiscuola.rai.it/percorsi/giornate
Role: discovery + educational/editorial topic ideas; not an exhaustive catalog.

C03 Pineta Nature Resort — Calendario Giornate Mondiali 2026
URL: https://www.pinetanatureresort.it/blog/giornate-mondiali/
Role: rich long-tail candidate discovery including popular/unofficial days.
Important: contains institutional, national, commercial/popular and humorous observances in the same editorial list; every candidate requires classification and verification.

C04 Wikipedia IT — Giornate, anni e decenni internazionali
URL: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Giornate,_anni_e_decenni_internazionali
Role: discovery/alias/resolution hints; follow citations to primary authority before publication.

PROCESS
