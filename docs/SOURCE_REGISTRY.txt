TIER D — ORIGINATOR / CONSUMER AUTHORITY
Used for non-UN consumer observances when the organization that created, coordinates or formally maintains the observance is known.
Examples in the first curated tranche: UICC, ITI, ICN, ICOM, American Tortoise Rescue, MSIF, Emojipedia/Zedge, IASP, ADI, World Heart Federation, The Vegan Society and World Kindness Movement.
A D_ORIGINATOR source can support RECOGNIZED_NGO or POPULAR classification. It must never be converted to OFFICIAL_UN/WHO/UNESCO.

PROCESS
DISCOVER -> SNAPSHOT -> NORMALIZE -> DEDUP HINTS -> VERIFY ORIGINATOR/PRIMARY -> CLASSIFY OFFICIALITY -> ADD CANONICAL RECORD -> GENERATE OCCURRENCES -> QA

AUTOMATION POLICY
The committed discovery backlog is validated in normal CI.
The live refresh workflow is manual/read-only and uploads a review artifact.
Live refreshes never commit directly to main and never promote a candidate automatically.

COPYRIGHT / CONTENT RULE
Use third-party pages to identify facts and candidates. Do not copy their prose, artwork or photos into World Days unless an explicit compatible license is recorded. World Days editorial copy is original and source-backed.
