## Remaining source-index and documentary risks

- UNESCO African Heritage and mangrove observances: distinguish the 2015 institutional proclamation from prior conservation initiatives; inaugural edition and precise historical choice of day may be `null`.
- UN language days: 2010 six-language programme is not the same as individually attested first annual ceremony; distinguish literary or legendary anniversaries from founding institutions.
- Day of the Seafarer: the 2010 Manila STCW conference instituted the day, while IMO's Assembly later endorsed in 2011 and first celebrations happened in 2011.
- International Day of Cooperatives: 1992/1994 formal UN resolutions, first UN observance in 1995 and earlier independent ICA tradition are distinct.
- International Week of Science and Peace: first nongovernmental celebrations 1986 preceded 1988 UN proclamation; the annual week includes 11 November.
- International Widows Day: Loomba Foundation 2005 precursor, 2010 UN resolution and first 2011 UN observance are separate, with 23 June tied to the founder's family history.
- World NTD Day: informal partner event 2020 came before formal WHA74(18) recognition in 2021.
- World Hearing Day: original Ear Care awareness initiative in 2007 predates current WHO campaign name; do not invent separate modern designation year.
- WIPO World IP Day: WIPO's 2000 records document Algeria and China proposing the 26 April date, linked to the WIPO Convention entering into force in 1970, while first observance was 2001.
- Country-specific 52 remaining slots require their *own* laws, original government records and legal/cultural distinction; an official indexed calendar is not enough.
