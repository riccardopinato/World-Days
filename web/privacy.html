World Days Privacy Policy

Effective date: 14 October 2026 · Last updated: 15 October 2026

Short version. World Days is designed as a local-first app and does not require an account. App preferences, favorites, recent activity, reminder settings and similar product data are stored primarily on your device. The mobile app can use Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) for advertising and privacy choices, Google Play Billing for the optional lifetime Remove Ads purchase, and GitHub Pages for verified catalog updates.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the World Days mobile application and its official web preview handle information. World Days helps users discover international and world observances, save favorites, configure reminders and browse editorial content.

2. Information stored by World Days

World Days currently does not require account registration or login. The app may store the following information locally on your device so its features can work:

  • language, theme, country/market and other app preferences;
  • favorites, recently viewed observances and local learning/usage state such as streak information;
  • notification preferences and reminder subscriptions;
  • local data used by the Android home-screen widget and notification scheduling;
  • locally generated share-card content when you choose to create or share it;
  • local Remove Ads entitlement state and, only when required to finish a store transaction reliably, a pending purchase-completion record containing the product identifier, transaction identifier and timestamp.

In the current release architecture, World Days does not operate a user account backend that uploads these local app preferences or favorites to a World Days server. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app can remove locally stored information.

3. Notifications and device features

If you enable reminders, World Days uses device notification capabilities to schedule local notifications. On Android, the app may request notification permission and use system restart/update signals so scheduled reminders and widget state can be restored after a reboot or app update. World Days does not currently use a proprietary remote-push account service for these reminders.

4. Advertising and consent

The production mobile app may display ads using Google Mobile Ads. World Days also integrates Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage privacy choices where required. Advertising is not requested until the consent/eligibility state reported by UMP allows an ad request.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK disclosures, the SDK can automatically collect and share data such as an IP address, product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for purposes including advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. The exact processing can depend on your privacy choices, device settings, location and Google's configuration.

You can review or change advertising privacy choices from Settings → Ads & privacy → Privacy choices when the UMP privacy-options entry point is required and available. You can also use privacy and advertising controls provided by your device or Google.

5. Purchases and Google Play Billing

On Android, World Days can offer an optional one-time lifetime Remove Ads product through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles the store account, payment method, purchase authorization and store-side transaction processing under Google's terms and privacy practices. World Days does not receive or store your payment-card details.

To provide and restore the entitlement, the app can receive store product information, purchase status, a transaction identifier and verification data from Google Play. World Days stores the resulting entitlement locally. If Google Play reports that a completed purchase still needs store acknowledgement/finalization, the app can temporarily store the product identifier, transaction identifier and timestamp locally so it can safely retry completion after a restart.

6. Web Preview, catalog updates and third-party links

The World Days Flutter Web Preview does not request AdMob ads by design. On supported mobile builds, World Days can make HTTPS requests to the official World Days GitHub Pages site to check for and download a public, versioned catalog update. These requests do not upload your favorites, recent history, reminder preferences or profile settings to GitHub. As with ordinary web requests, GitHub can receive standard network and diagnostic information such as the requesting IP address and request metadata under GitHub's own privacy terms.

Observance pages can contain links to external source or authority websites. Those websites are operated independently and their own privacy practices apply when you visit them.

This Privacy Policy page and the World Days Web Preview are hosted using GitHub Pages. Visiting pages hosted by GitHub can cause GitHub to receive standard web request and diagnostic information under GitHub's own privacy terms. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

7. Analytics

World Days currently has no separate first-party analytics backend enabled in the app. Advertising SDKs may nevertheless process measurement and diagnostic information as described above. If World Days later introduces additional analytics or a remote account/content service that materially changes data handling, this policy will be updated before that processing is treated as part of the production product.

8. Data sharing

World Days does not sell the local favorites, reminder preferences or profile settings described above. Information processed by third-party providers such as Google is governed by their terms, privacy policies, user choices and the configuration used by World Days.

9. Retention and deletion

Locally stored World Days data generally remains on the device until you remove it through app/system controls, clear app data, or uninstall the application. Retention of information processed by Google or GitHub is governed by those providers' applicable policies and controls.

10. Children

World Days is a general-audience informational app and is not designed specifically for children. The app does not require users to submit a name, email address or account profile to use its core features. Advertising configuration must follow the applicable age, audience and consent requirements for each release channel and market.

11. Security

World Days minimizes first-party data collection by keeping core product state local where practical. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, and third-party network services remain subject to their own security practices.

12. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when World Days changes its data practices, adds or removes third-party services, or needs to reflect legal or platform requirements. The current version will remain available at this URL with an updated effective date.

13. Contact

World Days is maintained through the official repository at github.com/riccardopinato/World-Days. For privacy-related questions, use the repository's contact channel and do not post sensitive personal information in a public issue.
